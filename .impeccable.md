# Design Context

## Project

ADK Agent Directory — a Next.js (App Router) site at `agentdirectory.folch.ai` that showcases AI agents built on Google's Agent Development Kit (ADK). Visitors browse a catalog, read agent details, and chat live (powered by Gemini 3 Flash).

## Users

- **Primary**: Developers and AI-curious folks evaluating agent patterns built on Google ADK. Often arriving from a Google search, a tweet, or a link in an ADK doc.
- **Secondary**: People who want to *use* the agents — try them in a chat, copy a prompt, share a result.
- **Context**: Mostly desktop, a meaningful share on mobile, daytime browsing, generally calm reading sessions (not high-pressure trading or oncall).

The job-to-be-done: "Show me what's possible with Google ADK, let me try it in 30 seconds, and let me figure out how it was built."

## Brand Personality

**Three words**: trustworthy, considered, quietly opinionated.

**Voice**: Confident but not flashy. Acts like a Google product engineer's personal portfolio — you can tell it was made by someone who knows the platform inside out. The "ADK" half is institutional and clean; the "folch.ai" half is the human signature underneath.

## Aesthetic Direction

- **Primary anchor**: Gemini app — generous radii, soft surfaces, calm motion, Material 3 sensibility, restrained color (Google Blue carries most of the chromatic weight).
- **Personal touch**: A distinct `folch.ai` wordmark in a serif italic, a signature accent color used sparingly alongside Google Blue, and a hand on the typography that's adjacent to Google Sans without being Inter.
- **Theme**: Light by default. Dark mode supported and tuned, but the canonical look is light.

### Anti-references — what this must NOT look like
- Generic AI startup (no purple→blue gradients, no neon glow, no glassmorphism, no "AI" badges).
- Sterile dashboard / admin UI (no equal-weight cards in endless grids, no border-left status stripes).
- Vercel-flavored dev tool (no monospace shorthand for "technical", no pure-black surfaces).
- Material 3 demo page (avoid the *templated* feel: every component a card, every card a shadow).

## Design Principles

1. **Subtractive over additive.** When in doubt, remove. The site shines because of what isn't there. Soft borders, restrained color, generous whitespace.
2. **Google Blue carries the brand; the accent is rare on purpose.** Primary is Google Blue (`oklch(0.55 0.22 264)` ish). The folch.ai accent — a warm signature — appears at most 1–2 times per screen.
3. **Type does the heavy lifting.** Two families only: a warm geometric sans (Figtree) for everything UI, and a serif italic (Source Serif 4) reserved for the wordmark and rare editorial moments.
4. **Surfaces, not boxes.** Avoid wrapping content in cards by default. Use spacing and tinted neutrals. Cards are reserved for content that genuinely groups (an agent tile, an artifact preview).
5. **Motion is calm, never bouncy.** ease-out-quart, transforms only, durations 150–250ms. The interface should feel like it's *settling* rather than performing.
